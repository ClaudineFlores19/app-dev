# My Favorite Movies/Series
### Below are some of my favorite movies and series 
**1. Legend of the Blue Sea -**
*&nbsp;A mermaid who travels to modern-day Seoul to reunite with her reincarnated love while their past lives unfold in parallel during the Joseon era*


**2. Love in the Moonlight -** 
*&nbsp;A Joseon Dynasty romance and political drama following Crown Prince Lee Yeong and Hong Ra-on, a young woman disguised as a male relationship advisor and later a palace eunuch.*


**3. Goblin -** 
*&nbsp;Follows Kim Shin, a 939-year-old immortal dokkaebi (goblin) in modern-day Seoul who must find his destined human bride to pull a sword from his chest and end his eternal life.*


**4. Five Feet Apart -** 
*&nbsp;Follows two teenagers with cystic fibrosis—Stella Grant and Will Newman—who fall in love in the hospital while medical rules force them to stay apart to prevent deadly cross-infection.*

**5. Midnight Sun -** 
*&nbsp;Follows 17-year-old Katie Price, who has a rare, life-threatening sensitivity to sunlight (Xeroderma Pigmentosum) and must stay indoors during the day. At night, she goes out to play guitar and begins a romance with Charlie, her longtime crush, while hiding her condition from him*
